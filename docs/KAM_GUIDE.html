Promo Uplift Dashboard

A plain-language guide to reading your promotion results — no technical knowledge required.

What does this tool do?

When you run a promotion on a casino site, the big question is: did it actually move stakes?

This dashboard gives you that answer for every promotion in one place. For each promo, it takes the total stakes wagered during the promotion and compares them to the same number of days immediately before it started — at the same site, for the same games. The percentage difference is the uplift.

Understanding uplift

Uplift tells you whether stakes went up or down during the promotion, relative to the period just before it.

+32.1% Positive Stakes were 32% higher during the promo than in the comparison window. The promotion worked.
−21.4% Negative Stakes were lower during the promo. The promotion may not have had the intended effect.
N/A Unmeasurable The result can't be calculated reliably. See the N/A section below for why.

The Impact (€) column shows the same result in euros — the raw euro difference between during and before. Use this number when reporting business impact to management; a percentage alone loses context without knowing the size of the baseline.

Column reference

ColumnWhat it shows
PromoThe promo ID from your records, e.g. P01
SiteThe casino site where the promotion ran
GamesWhich games were included. ALL games means every game at that site was part of the promo
DaysHow many days the promotion ran
Before (€)Total stakes in the same number of days immediately before the promo started — this is the baseline the uplift is measured against
During (€)Total stakes during the promotion itself
Impact (€)During minus Before, in euros. Green means a gain; red means a loss. Use this in reports to management
UpliftThe percentage change: (During − Before) ÷ Before × 100. Green is positive, red is negative, grey is N/A

The ⚠ warning icon

⚠
Low baseline — treat with caution

If you see a small warning icon next to a Before (€) figure, it means the baseline was under €5,000. A large uplift percentage on a small baseline can be misleading. Going from €500 to €5,000 is a +900% uplift on paper, but it may simply be normal day-to-day variation. These results are real numbers — but present them carefully and provide context before including them in a report.

What does N/A mean?

Some promotions cannot be fairly measured. When you see N/A in the Uplift column, the text below the badge explains why. Common reasons:

Data entry error

"End date is before start date" — the promotion end date appears to be entered incorrectly in the system. The data team needs to correct the entry before this can be calculated.

No baseline data

"No stakes in the baseline period" — there were no recorded stakes for those games at that site in the comparison window. Without a baseline, there is nothing to compare against.

Unmeasurable promos are hidden by default so they don't clutter your view. Use the "show N unmeasurable" link above the table to make them visible when you need to investigate them.

Filtering and sorting

1

Search box — type any part of a promo ID, site name, or game name. The table filters instantly as you type.

2

Column headers are sortable — click any column header to sort by that column. Click again to reverse the order. The table opens sorted by Uplift (highest first) so your best-performing promos are always at the top.

3

Show/hide unmeasurable — use the link above the table to toggle promos that can't be measured. They're hidden by default to keep your view clean.

Things to keep in mind

The baseline matches the promo length. A 7-day promo is compared to the 7 days immediately before it; a 14-day promo to the 14 days before it. The comparison period always ends the day before the promo started.
Comparisons are site-and-game specific. A promo on Golden Fox at Casino Alpha is only compared to Golden Fox at Casino Alpha — not to other games or other sites.
Seasonality is not adjusted for. If the promo ran over a public holiday and the baseline was all normal weekdays, the comparison may overstate the effect. Use your judgement when the timing looks unusual.
Overlapping promos are not flagged automatically. If two promos ran close together at the same site, the baseline of the second one may include activity from the first. Check the dates manually in those cases.
💡
Tip for reporting

Sort by Impact (€) descending to find the promos that moved the most money — not just the highest percentage. A +15% uplift on a €200k baseline is a much bigger story than a +200% uplift on a €500 one.
